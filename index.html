El Pollo Loco

Meet the Developer

Hi there! I'm a passionate developer who loves turning complex logic into interactive experiences. My latest project, El Pollo Loco, was to push my technical boundaries and see just how much I could accomplish in a view days.

About the Project

El Pollo Loco is a fast-paced browser game developed in just 15 days. The assets and graphics are from the talented design team at the Developer Akademie.

What I Learned

This project was more than just a race against the clock. It was a deep dive into the world of Object-Oriented Programming (OOP). I learned much about Classes and Inheritance, Encapsulation and Maintainable Architecture. Stepping away from simple scripts and moving into a structured, object-oriented mindset was a big "level up" of this journey. Enjoy the game!
